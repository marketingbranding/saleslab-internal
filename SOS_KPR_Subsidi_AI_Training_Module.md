# Solution Oriented Selling (SOS) for Government Subsidized Housing Sales

**Version:** 1.0\
**Purpose:** Knowledge Base for AI Roleplay & Sales Evaluation

## Overview

This module adapts the original Solution Oriented Selling (SOS)
framework into a domain-specific methodology for government subsidized
housing (KPR Subsidi). The objective is not only to train salespeople,
but also to serve as the primary knowledge base for an AI roleplay and
evaluation system.

Core philosophy:

> Do not sell houses. Help customers successfully obtain and own their
> first home.

A successful salesperson is measured by successful customer
outcomes---not by the number of bookings alone.

Success journey:

Booking → Complete Documents → Bank Review → SP3K → Akad → Handover →
Referral

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 1 --- Sales Mindset

Traditional sales focuses on products.

Solution Oriented Selling focuses on customer success.

## Traditional

-   Explain product features
-   Push promotions
-   Chase bookings

## Solution Oriented

-   Understand customer goals
-   Identify eligibility
-   Recommend the correct solution
-   Guide customers until Akad

AI Evaluation:

The AI should reward salespeople who prioritize customer suitability
over aggressive selling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 2 --- Customer Journey

Stages:

1.  Awareness
2.  Lead
3.  Inquiry
4.  Project Visit / Survey
5.  Booking Fee
6.  Document Collection
7.  Bank Submission
8.  SP3K
9.  Akad
10. Handover
11. Referral

Rule:

Never attempt to close beyond the customer's current stage.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 3 --- Prospect Qualification

## HOT

-   Eligible
-   Ready documents
-   Stable income
-   Serious buyer

## WARM

-   Interested
-   Still discussing with family
-   Saving for down payment

## COLD

-   Not eligible
-   No purchasing plan
-   Information gathering only

AI should classify every persona into one of these groups.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 4 --- HOME Probing Framework

Instead of generic SPIN, use HOME.

## H --- Housing

-   Already own a house?
-   Renting?
-   Living with parents?

## O --- Occupation

-   Job
-   Employment duration
-   Employer

## M --- Money

-   Monthly income
-   Additional income
-   Existing loans

## E --- Eligibility

-   Ever received housing subsidy?
-   SLIK status
-   Married?
-   Indonesian citizen?

Missing critical questions reduce evaluation scores.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 5 --- Eligibility Checklist

Sales should verify:

-   Indonesian citizen
-   Never owned a house
-   Never received subsidy
-   Income within latest regulation
-   Clean SLIK
-   Sufficient employment history
-   Complete documents

The AI should identify unanswered eligibility items.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 6 --- Presenting Solutions

Always connect needs with benefits.

Feature: \> Fixed monthly installment.

Benefit: \> Monthly housing payment builds ownership instead of paying
rent forever.

The AI should distinguish Feature, Advantage, and Benefit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 7 --- Objection Handling Database

Common objections:

-   Afraid of bank rejection
-   Freelancer income
-   No NPWP
-   Not married
-   Need spouse approval
-   Need family discussion
-   Installment concern
-   Comparing projects
-   Wants to postpone

Evaluation:

Sales should explore the reason before responding.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 8 --- Negotiation

Government subsidized housing generally does not negotiate selling
price.

Negotiation topics include:

-   Survey schedule
-   Booking date
-   Document submission
-   Bank process
-   Bonus (if available)
-   Handover schedule

Never promise discounts beyond company policy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module 9 --- Closing

Soft Closing

"Which month would you like to move?"

Booking Closing

"Shall we reserve your unit today?"

Document Closing

"Let's prepare your documents."

Bank Closing

"We'll submit your application."

Akad Closing

"Now we prepare for signing."

The AI should determine whether the closing matches the customer's
readiness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
