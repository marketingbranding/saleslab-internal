# AI Roleplay Training Simulator PRD

## Product Overview

### Vision

Build a voice-first AI roleplay training platform that feels like a
retro computer simulation rather than a CRM. Users enter training
missions, speak with AI over a phone-call interface, and receive
detailed post-mission analysis.

## Goals

-   Train communication skills through AI voice conversations.
-   Encourage repeated practice through game mechanics.
-   Allow administrators to create new scenarios without code.
-   Track measurable improvement over time.

## Target Users

### End Users

-   Sales
-   Marketing
-   Customer Service
-   Recruiters
-   Students

### Administrators

-   Manage users
-   Create scenarios
-   Configure personas
-   Review analytics
-   Configure AI prompts

## Design Philosophy

The product should resemble an old training terminal with modern
usability.

Inspirations: - Windows 95 - Metal Gear Codec - Fallout terminals -
Persona menus - CRT monitors

Avoid: - CRM appearance - Pixel art - Glassmorphism - Chat-style
interfaces
